## Quarter 2 Project Brainstorm

**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic we have covered this quarter is the process of estimating what stats low-minutes players would achieve if they were given more minutes. We discussed how optimal transport could be used to find the optimal way to transport the stat distribution of low-minutes players to the distribution of high-minutes players.

A potential change we'd like to make to our Quarter 1 project is to take a more advanced approach to event value modeling, and to spend more time engineering stats from our event data that cover the quality indexes we are trying to create to evaluate players.
